Product architecture

Stock Lookup hiện tại

Stock Lookup is an evidence-first Vietnamese-market research product. Python/deterministic contracts are the numerical authority; this Dashboard is a static rendering layer for retained product artifacts.

Structured evidence → deterministic financial features → Tactical V2 → liquidity/risk and explicit portfolio context → valuation/opportunity → security decision → Investment Decision Workspace → prospective outcome measurement.

What the visible surfaces do
  • Tổng quan: what is happening and entry points.
  • Bộ lọc: find a ticker, then open its decision card.
  • Phân tích: compare retained research axes and conflicts.
  • Tín hiệu: Tactical V2 state, confirmation and invalidation.
  • Không gian quyết định: thẻ quyết định bảy phần.
  • Danh mục: explicit browser-local input and research context.
Important limits
  • Research stance is not an execution order.
  • Proxy, qualified, unavailable and stale dimensions remain distinct.
  • No opaque total score, weighted investment ranking, target price, probability, or exact execution sizing is rendered as current product authority.
  • Freshness is retained per axis; a usable financial or catalyst period is not silently relabelled as today’s market session.
  • Prospective outcomes begin only after a genuine retained T0 case; historical artifacts are not promoted retroactively.
Static dashboard content is research-only and for reference. It does not expose private data, credentials, or implementation secrets.
